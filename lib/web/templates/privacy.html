Privacy

goals stores only what it needs to work: your phone number, your timezone and reminder time, your goals, and your daily answers.

  • Texts and login codes are sent through Twilio, which processes your number to deliver them.
  • Your mobile number and text opt-in will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • A cookie keeps you logged in for 30 days. There is no tracking.
  • Deleting your account in Settings removes all of your data right away.

Questions: github.com/icco/goals/issues.
