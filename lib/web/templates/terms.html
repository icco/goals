Terms

goals is a free service that texts you once a day to ask whether you did your daily goals, and keeps a record of your answers.

Text messages

  • Program: goals daily check-ins.
  • What you get: a login code when you sign in, one reminder a day at the time you choose, and a short reply when you answer.
  • Frequency: about one message a day. Message frequency varies.
  • Cost: goals is free. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Stop: reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Help: reply HELP, or open an issue at github.com/icco/goals/issues.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your account

You can pause texts or delete your account at any time from Settings. goals is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or shut down.

See also the Privacy Policy.
